Day 3 • Tuesday, April 1

Todra Gorge

Into the mountains

The Experience

Morocco's Grand Canyon

After our spectacular drive through the Atlas Mountains, we arrive at one of Morocco's most dramatic natural wonders—towering canyon walls that glow orange in the afternoon sun.

Todra Gorge is where the High Atlas makes its final statement. A river has carved a narrow passage through limestone cliffs that rise 300 meters (nearly 1,000 feet) straight up. The walls are so close at points you can touch both sides.

Quick Facts

📅 Date: Tuesday, April 1, 2026
🏨 Nights: 1
💰 Cost: £37 (Hotel AZUL)
🌡️ Weather: 22°C/72°F perfect
⛰️ Gorge height: 300m walls
📸 Best time: Late afternoon light

Where We're Staying

Hotel AZUL

Simple, comfortable, perfectly located. Hotel AZUL sits right at the entrance to the gorge—walk 5 minutes and you're in the canyon. Great value at £37, clean rooms, helpful staff, and exactly what we need after a long driving day.

This isn't luxury—it's authentic mountain Morocco. The focus here is the GORGE, not the hotel. We're here to experience nature's drama, rest up, and prepare for the desert tomorrow.

What We'll Do

Afternoon & Evening in the Gorge

🏔️ Walk the Gorge

The main activity: walk into the canyon as the afternoon light illuminates the orange walls. Rock climbers scale the faces (spectacular to watch). The path is easy, flat, tourist-friendly—but the drama is REAL.

📸 Photography

Late afternoon = magic hour. The sun lights up one wall while the other stays in shadow. Every angle is stunning.

🍽️ Evening

Dinner at hotel or nearby restaurants. Traditional tagine, mountain air, early night. We've driven 5.5 hours and have another 2.5 hours to the desert tomorrow.

😌 The Vibe

Quiet mountain town. Tourist presence but low-key. This is adventure Morocco—nature focus, simple comforts.

Why One Night Is Perfect

Todra Gorge is spectacular but small. The main gorge walk takes 1-2 hours. We arrive Tuesday afternoon (~4pm after 5.5h drive), explore the gorge, have dinner, sleep, and leave Wednesday morning for Merzouga.

This is a scenic stopover between Marrakech's chaos and the desert's vastness—and the gorge provides the perfect mountain drama to bridge them.
