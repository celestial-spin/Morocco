How to Claim

While abroad: For medical emergencies, call the 24/7 line immediately (within 48 hours of any hospitalisation). For everything else (baggage, delays, theft), just gather evidence — receipts, photos, police reports, airline documentation. You do NOT need to file the claim while abroad.

When home: File claims online through the Allianz Assistance Travel Insurance hub (access via NatWest app or online banking), or call/email. No hard deadline, but submit as soon as reasonably possible — sooner is better while evidence is fresh.

Car rental excess: If damage occurs, pay the excess to the rental company, then claim it back from Allianz. Keep all paperwork — rental agreement, damage report, payment receipt.

Key rule: Keep ALL receipts and documentation. Take photos of everything. Get written reports wherever possible. Without documentation, claims cannot be processed.
