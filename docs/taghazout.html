The Experience

Three Nights of Pure Relaxation

After mountains, desert, kasbahs, and 1,100km of driving—we've earned THIS. Three nights at the Hyatt Regency with ocean views, gym, spa, pools, and zero agenda.

🌊 Taghazout Village

Laid-back surf town with chill cafes, yoga studios, and fresh seafood restaurants. We can walk to the village (15 mins) or just stay at the Hyatt—both options perfect.

The vibe: Bohemian surfers, international travelers, locals fishing, gentle waves, colorful boats. Completely different from everywhere else on this trip!
