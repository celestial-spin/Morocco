Drive Section 1 (35km, 30min)

10:00-10:30 → Rissani
10:30-11:30 FOSSIL SHOPPING: Mezane Fossil Store
• On main road between Erfoud/Rissani (directly on route!)
• Family business, fair prices, workshop tour
• Browse fossils, trilobites, meteorites (see Fossils Guide)
• Budget: £60-150 for nice souvenirs

Lunch Stop

11:30-12:30 Lunch in Rissani
Option 1: Café Restaurant Amraoui (Recommended!)
• Location: Just outside Rissani center (on route)
• Famous for: Madfouna (Berber pizza - local specialty!)
• Also good: Kefta tagine, Moroccan salads
• Price: ~85 MAD (£7) per person
• English-speaking, tourist-friendly

Option 2: Quick snacks/picnic supplies in Rissani
• Buy fresh bread, cheese, olives, fruit
• Eat scenic picnic stop later (palmeries en route)
• Saves time, more flexible

Optional Museum Visit

If time permits: Tahiri Fossil Museum
• Location: 11km north on Erfoud-Rissani road (15 min each way)
• Hours: 7am-sunset daily • Entry: FREE (donations welcome)
• Duration: 30-45 min visit • Giant dinosaur replicas outside!
• Total time addition: +1.5 hours (30min drive + 45min visit)
• Only add if ahead of schedule - pool time is priority!
